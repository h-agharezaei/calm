Sometimes calming down starts with a change of scale: looking at something far bigger than the day's worries. In this meditation, Calm's background becomes a galaxy turning very slowly, while the deep, drawn-out song of a whale plays in the background.

## The story of the 52 Hz whale

The "52 Hz whale" is the name given to an unidentified whale whose calls have been recorded in the Pacific Ocean since the late 1980s. Its voice sits at around 52 hertz, higher than most whales of its kind, which is why it's sometimes called "the loneliest whale in the world", although scientists still aren't sure about it.

The sound you hear in this session is a calm, slowly changing song that works well as a background for silence without pulling your attention to itself.

## What's in this session

- **Length:** 10 minutes, with a 5-second get-ready countdown and a soft bell at the start and end
- **Sound:** the 52 Hz whale song, with adjustable volume
- **Visual:** a spinning galaxy; with reduced motion turned on, it stays still
- **Price:** free, no sign-up

## How to practice

1. Sit or lie down comfortably.
2. Look at the center of the galaxy for a few seconds, then close your eyes if you like.
3. Rest your attention on the whale song: where it begins, where it fades, and the silence between calls.
4. When your mind drifts to something else, just notice it and return to the sound.

Focusing on a single sound like this is one of the simplest forms of meditation. If you'd rather work with your breath, try [box breathing](/en/blog/box-breathing/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Does a 52 Hz sound have any special effect?
We don't make any claims about the effects of this frequency. The sound was chosen because it's calm and steady, which makes it a good background for meditation.

### Can I change the sound or the visual?
Yes. In Calm you can switch the background sound to ocean, river, forest, fire or 432 Hz music, and choose between nature photos, a kaleidoscope, the galaxy or the ocean.

### What if I fall asleep?
Nothing happens. After ten minutes the closing bell plays softly. If your goal is sleep, you can choose a longer session.
