This meditation is for the moments when you only have ten minutes and want a short break from a busy day. Press start and Calm opens with the sound of ocean waves, while the background turns into a quiet sea at night: moonlight on the water, stars, thin drifting clouds and, now and then, a shooting star.

After a five-second get-ready countdown, a soft bell marks the beginning, and another one marks the end ten minutes later. You don't need to keep an eye on the clock.

## What's in this session

- **Length:** 10 minutes, with a gentle bell at the start and at the end
- **Sound:** continuous ocean waves, with adjustable volume
- **Visual:** an animated night ocean; if you have reduced motion turned on, it stays still
- **Price:** free, no sign-up, nothing to install

## Before you start

1. Sit somewhere you won't be disturbed for a few minutes. A chair, a sofa or the floor all work; just be comfortable.
2. Use headphones if you have them. The waves sound fuller and closer.
3. Put your phone on silent or Do Not Disturb.

## What to do during the ten minutes

The simplest approach is to rest your attention on the sound of the waves. The waves come in and go out; let your breath do the same, without trying to control it.

Every few seconds your mind will wander: tomorrow's work, a message you haven't answered, anything at all. That's completely normal and doesn't mean you're doing it wrong. Each time you notice, gently come back to the sound of the waves, without judging yourself. Coming back *is* the practice.

If you prefer to keep your eyes open, watch the moonlight on the water. With your eyes closed, the sound of the sea is enough.

> Tip: you don't need to "empty" your mind. Just notice where it went, and come back.

## When the bell rings

Don't rush. Take a few normal breaths, open your eyes slowly if they were closed, and before you get up, take a moment to notice how your body feels right now.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need any meditation experience?
No. This session works for complete beginners. If you've never meditated before, read [how to meditate for beginners](/en/blog/meditation-for-beginners/) as well.

### Can I change the length?
Yes. Once Calm is open, the meditation panel lets you pick 15, 30 or 60 minutes or any custom length, and switch the background sound.

### Does it work offline?
Calm can be installed on your phone and the app itself opens offline. You can also save the nature sounds for offline use from the settings.

### When is the best time for this meditation?
Whenever you can actually do it. Many people use it in the evening before bed, or as a short break in the middle of the day. Doing it regularly matters more than the time of day.
