Box breathing is one of the simplest breathing exercises there is. The name comes from the shape of a square: four equal sides, meaning four steps of equal length. It's also known as **4-4-4-4 breathing** or **square breathing**.

Many people use it for a short pause before an important meeting, in the middle of a hectic day or before going to sleep, because it's easy to learn and needs no equipment.

## The four steps of box breathing

1. **Inhale (4 seconds):** breathe in slowly through your nose, counting to four.
2. **Hold (4 seconds):** hold the breath without straining, counting to four.
3. **Exhale (4 seconds):** breathe out slowly through your nose or mouth, counting to four.
4. **Pause (4 seconds):** with your lungs empty, count to four again.

Those four steps are one round. Four to eight rounds is usually plenty to start with.

## Tips for better practice

- **Sit upright and relaxed.** Let your shoulders drop and unclench your jaw.
- **Don't force it.** If four seconds feels like too much, start with three. What matters is keeping the steps equal, not the number.
- **Keep the count slow and steady.** It helps keep the rhythm even.
- **If you feel dizzy or uncomfortable,** stop and breathe normally.

## Box breathing compared with other techniques

Calm includes other patterns too. In **4-7-8 breathing**, for example, the exhale is longer than the inhale, and in **coherent breathing** you breathe in and out for about 5.5 seconds each, with no holds. Box breathing is easier to start with because its rhythm is even and easy to remember.

## Frequently asked questions

### How often should I do box breathing?
There's no fixed rule. You can do a few rounds whenever you need a short pause.

### Should I breathe through my nose or mouth?
The inhale is usually done through the nose. For the exhale, use whichever is more comfortable.

### What if I can't hold my breath for four seconds?
Shorten the count, for example three seconds per step. In Calm you can also use the "Custom" pattern to set the length of each step yourself.
