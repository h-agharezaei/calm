## LinkedIn (en)

"4-7-8 breathing will put you to sleep in 60 seconds." You've probably seen some version of that claim.

The research is more modest. Studies of the technique itself are few and small, and none of them shows that it improves sleep. Slow breathing in general has more evidence behind it, with real limitations of its own.

Still, it's a simple, free way to take a pause. The practical part most guides skip:
1. The numbers are a ratio, not exact seconds. If a 7-second hold feels hard, count faster.
2. Start with four rounds. More rounds early on is more likely to make you lightheaded than calm.
3. The hold should never feel like straining.

If you have a heart or lung condition or you're pregnant, check with your doctor before practicing breath holds.

The full guide covers the steps, common mistakes, how it compares to box breathing, and the studies behind each claim.
