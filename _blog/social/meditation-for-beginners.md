## LinkedIn (en)

Most people who try meditation and quit share one misconception: that they're supposed to empty their mind.

In a well-known study (Killingsworth & Gilbert, Science), people's minds were elsewhere in close to half of the moments they were asked about. A wandering mind is the default, not a personal failure.

Meditation doesn't ask you to stop that. It asks for something simpler:
1. Pick an anchor, like the feeling of the breath.
2. When you notice your mind has drifted, label it: "thinking", "planning".
3. Gently come back.

That coming back is the practice. Five minutes a day beats an hour once a week.

The full beginner's guide covers the main types, common mistakes and an honest summary of what the research does and doesn't show: meditation is an attention skill, not a treatment.
